> Learn how to view, respond to, escalate, and resolve merchant support tickets from the Zid Partner Dashboard.
When a merchant needs help with your app or theme, Zid assigns the support ticket to you in the **Support Tickets** section of the Partner Dashboard.

This guide explains how to view tickets, communicate with merchants, escalate issues to Zid, and resolve support requests.

## **Access Support Tickets**

The **Support Tickets** page is available to partners with at least one **published app or theme**.

**Note:** If you have a published app or theme but no assigned support tickets, the page displays **"No tickets yet"**. Tickets will appear once Zid assigns merchant support requests to you.

**Note:** Shipping app tickets are handled directly by Zid's team and do not appear in the partner's Support Tickets page.

To view your tickets:

- Sign in to your **Partner Dashboard**.

- Select **Support Tickets** from the dashboard menu.

- Select a ticket to view its details and conversation.

## **Understand ticket stages**

Each ticket moves through different stages depending on its progress.

**Stage**

**Description**

**New**

Zid has assigned the ticket to you, and it requires your attention.

**In progress**

You have replied to the merchant and are working on the issue.

**Sent to Zid**

The ticket has been escalated to Zid's team for assistance.

**Resolved - awaiting merchant confirmation**

You submitted a resolution, and the merchant must confirm or reject it.

**Closed**

The merchant confirmed the resolution, or Zid closed the ticket.

## **Navigate the Support Tickets board**

The board provides an overview of tickets assigned to you.

### **Ticket summary**

Three summary counts appear at the top:

- **Need your action:** Tickets requiring your attention.

- **Waiting on merchant:** Tickets awaiting merchant confirmation.

- **Closed:** Tickets that have been closed.

Select a summary to filter the board. Select it again to view all tickets.

### **Filter tickets**

Under **Filter tickets**, you can filter by:

- **Store ID:** Enter the exact store ID.

- **App or Theme:** View tickets associated with a specific app or theme.

Select **Clear filters** to remove filters.

Filters are saved in the page URL, allowing you to bookmark or share a filtered view with another team member using the same partner account.

### **View ticket cards**

Each ticket card displays the app or theme name, store name, and creation date.

Tickets in **New** and **In progress** also show how long they have been assigned to you.

New and In progress tickets appear oldest first. Other stages display the five latest tickets, with a **Show all** option to view more.

**Note:** On mobile devices, ticket stages appear as tabs.

### **View ticket details**

Select any ticket to open its details.

The ticket page includes:

- **Ticket number:** A unique reference number you can copy when contacting Zid.

- **Status:** The current ticket stage.

- **Ticket facts:** The app or theme name and issue type.

- **Store & merchant:** Store name, ID, URL, and merchant contact details.

- **Issue description:** The original issue reported by the merchant, including attachments.

- **Conversation:** All messages exchanged between the merchant, your team, and Zid.

Conversation messages are labeled **Merchant**, **You**, or **Zid team**. Internal notes are highlighted separately.

If the merchant is waiting for a response, a notice appears with the time of their latest message.

## **Reply to a merchant**

To respond to a support ticket:

- Open the ticket.

- Select **Merchant response** in the conversation area.

- Write your response (up to 5,000 characters).

- Optionally, select **Attach file** to include supporting documents. Each file must not exceed **8 MB**.

- Select **Send to merchant**.

Your response becomes visible to the merchant in their Zid dashboard.

When you reply to a **New** ticket, it automatically moves to **In progress**.

**Note:** You can send merchant responses at any stage except **Closed**.

### **Add an internal note**

Internal notes allow you to share information with your team and Zid without displaying it to the merchant.

- Open the ticket.

- Select **Internal note**.

- Enter your note.

- Select **Add internal note**.

**Important:** Internal notes are not visible to merchants and cannot contain attachments. You can add internal notes even after a ticket is closed.

## **Change a ticket's status**

Use the status dropdown at the top of the ticket page to take action.

**Action**

**Available stages**

**In progress**

New

**Sent to Zid**

New, In progress

**Resolved - awaiting merchant confirmation**

New, In progress

Selecting **In progress** opens the merchant response composer. The status updates when you send your reply.

The other options open the relevant escalation or resolution form. The status changes only after you submit the form.

## **Send a ticket to Zid**

Use this option when an issue requires assistance or permissions from Zid's team.

- Open the ticket's status dropdown.

- Select **Sent to Zid**.

- Complete the **Send ticket to Zid** form:

- **Reason for sending to Zid:** Select Technical issue from Zid or Action requiring Zid permissions .

- **What have you checked or tried?** Describe the troubleshooting steps performed (up to 3,000 characters).

- **Problem description & evidence:** Explain the issue, when it started, and any relevant order numbers (up to 3,000 characters).

- **Evidence files:** Upload at least one file, up to five files, with a maximum size of **8 MB per file**. Supported evidence includes screenshots, screen recordings, PDFs, and HAR/log files.

- Select **Send to Zid**.

The ticket moves to **Sent to Zid**, and Zid's team takes over.

Your submitted information remains available under **Summary sent to Zid**.

## **Resolve a ticket**

When you have addressed the merchant's issue:

- Open the status dropdown and select **Resolved - awaiting merchant confirmation**.

- Complete the **Resolve ticket** form:

- **Resolution message to the merchant:** Explain the issue, what was fixed, and any actions required from the merchant (20–5,000 characters).

- **Issue type:** Select Bug in our app , Merchant configuration , How-to / usage , Not supported , Third-party service , or No merchant reply .

- **Ticket classification:** Select **Issue** or **Request**.

- Select **Resolve & notify merchant**.

The ticket moves to **Resolved - awaiting merchant confirmation**.

Your resolution message appears under **Proposed resolution**.

## **Merchant confirmation**

After you submit a resolution, the merchant receives a **WhatsApp form** to confirm or reject it.

- **If the merchant confirms:** The ticket moves to **Closed**.

- **If the merchant rejects:** The ticket returns to **In progress**, allowing you to continue working on it.

You cannot close a ticket directly. Closure happens after merchant confirmation or an action by Zid.

## **Move to the next ticket**

After replying to or resolving a ticket, select **Next ticket** to open the next ticket requiring your attention.

Tickets are prioritized according to how long they have been waiting.

## **View and download attachments**

Files shared in the ticket conversation include:

- **Preview:** View supported images and PDFs directly within the ticket.

- **Download:** Save the file to your device using its original filename.

Other supported file types can be downloaded but may not be previewed.

## **Frequently asked questions**

### **Why does my Support Tickets page show "No tickets yet"?**

This message appears when you have at least one published app or theme but no support tickets have been assigned to you yet.

### Why don't shipping app tickets appear?

Shipping app support requests are handled directly by Zid's team.

### How will I know when a new ticket is assigned?

An automatic email notification is sent to the **Zid Communication Email** configured in your partner account profile.

### Can I close a ticket myself?

No. You can submit a resolution, but the ticket closes only after the merchant confirms it or Zid closes the ticket.

### What happens if the merchant rejects my resolution?

The ticket returns to **In progress**, and you can continue assisting the merchant.

### Can merchants view internal notes?

No. Internal notes are visible only to your team and Zid.
