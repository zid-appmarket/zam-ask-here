> This article explores the two types of applications in the Zid App Market: Public/Private Apps and Shipping Apps.
## **App types**

In the Zid App Market, we support two types of Applications: Public/private Applications, and Shipping Applications. Each has a specific cycle you must follow to achieve a successful integration.

## 2. **Shipping Application**

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/2767514937/a9b651e0e13028341aae2db76923/image.png?expires=1794346200&signature=653b98638ce97b9b3b182915f622d777f077a6db6761547a74adf6a94e753b38&req=dichEcx%2FmYhcXvMW1HO4zSgUCIbTo1Ueb0nDjaxyu%2BF%2F62HzT3LJ5gM8g5jw%0AEaRW9wRB%2BJRH1lbONzM%3D%0A)

This type includes any logistics services such as:

- Shipping

- Fulfillment

- Dark stores

Shipping applications are listed under **Shipping & Fulfilment** on the public Zid App Market (https://apps.zid.sa/). In the merchant dashboard, shipping applications appear on the dedicated **Shipping** page rather than in the App Market application list.

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/2767507764/a35a6121067dcf8254574e92fc06/image.png?expires=1794346200&signature=8c16e6b8e184f55d35cba318817596a444b4062a8720d6e7645e74778e6cacc7&req=dichEcx%2BmoZZXfMW1HO4zR9TLubuxjsnNRQHwDiNCeDImMS3gGnd1mrkBfzl%0AlrhGef%2FIT%2BmjSPbRR1c%3D%0A)

Embedded applications are now identified with an **"Embedded"** tag on their app cards in the Zid App Market, replacing the previous separate embedded apps section.

The tag appears automatically based on the existing embedded app (https://docs.zid.sa/embedded-apps-2168216m0) setting configured in the Partner Dashboard.

**Note:** No additional configuration or action is required from partners.

## **App Appearance options**

As a developer, you can publish applications in two ways: **Public and Private** Applications; under the hood, both types are the same. However, the only difference is the listing status.

### 1. **Public application:**

Will be published on the market for all of our merchants, so any merchant can view it and install it.

### **2. Private application:**

A private application is created for **one specific Zid store** and is not listed publicly in Zid App Market.

- Partners can create a private app directly from the **Partner Dashboard (https://partners.zid.sa/)**https://partners.zid.sa/by selecting **Private**, entering the target **Store ID**, and sharing the generated activation link with the merchant.

- The merchant activates the app directly through the standard OAuth consent flow. No Zid review or manual installation is required.

For complete setup and activation details, see **Create a private app (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/17348249-create-a-private-app)**.
