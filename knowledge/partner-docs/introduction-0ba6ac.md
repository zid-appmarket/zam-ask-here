# Introduction

A **Zid theme** is more than a design, it’s the full presentation layer of a merchant’s storefront.

Each theme combines:

**Jinja templates** that map storefront routes (home, product, cart, etc.).

**HTML, CSS, and JavaScript assets** for layout and interactions.

**JSON schemas** to expose customizable settings in the Theme Editor.

🚀

Once packaged, a single theme can be installed and sold across thousands of Zid stores, giving merchants a professional, customizable starting point for their online business.

## Why Build & Sell on Zid? #

Zid powers **tens of thousands of merchants across MENA**, offering you direct access to growing businesses that need professional storefronts.

Building a theme here means:

**Large customer base** — reach merchants in multiple industries.

**Recurring revenue** — themes are licensed and sold across stores.

**Partner visibility** — your theme is marketed through Zid’s ecosystem.

**Developer support** — access to docs, sample themes, and a dedicated team.

Browse live themes available in the **Zid Theme Market (https://themes.zid.sa/)** to see how layouts, industries, and design approaches come to life.

## Industries & Categories #

Zid merchants operate in a wide variety of industries. Popular categories include:

👗 **Fashion & Apparel**

💄 **Beauty & Cosmetics**

🍔 **Food & Beverages**

📱 **Electronics & Gadgets**

🛋 **Home & Lifestyle**

🏋️ **Sports & Fitness**

When designing a theme, think about **industry-specific needs**:

Food themes → strong visuals, delivery info.

Fashion themes → large product galleries, size variants.

Electronics → detailed product specs & comparison.

## Key Metrics to Consider #

When looking for your next theme idea, analyze merchant demand and adoption:

**Most installed themes** (see marketplace trends).

**Conversion drivers** — themes with fast load times & mobile-first design perform better.

**Customization needs** — merchants prefer flexible homepage layouts & configurable sections.

**Industry fit** — niches like food delivery or fashion often dominate in adoption.
