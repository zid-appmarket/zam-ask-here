# 🚀 Start Here

As a Zid Partner, you have the opportunity to create and offer apps and themes that help merchants scale their businesses. Whether you’re a developer, designer, or tech entrepreneur, we provide you with the ideal platform to bring your innovative solutions to a growing network of online stores.

Your Guide to Building Better

Explore the Merchant's help center (https://help.zid.sa/) to understand the dashboard features and configure your testing environment.

## Join as a Zid Partner #

Your partnership journey begins by registering your partner account on Zid. Once registered, you will have access to the Partner Dashboard, where you can manage your apps and themes, track subscribed stores, monitor your monthly payouts and more!

### Unlock Growth and Opportunities! #

Joining Zid as a partner opens the door to numerous benefits, including:

**Expand Your Reach:** Connect with a broad network of merchants eager to enhance their stores.

**Unlock Revenue Streams:** Generate income through app sales, theme designs, and additional services.

**Comprehensive Support & Resources:** Access a wealth of developer tools, technical assistance, and marketing resources to help you succeed.
