# Steps to Submit Your Theme for Review

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/1545483328/aa2542b200529e2cf4da3182e37e/image%2B-2811-29.png?expires=1794346200&signature=3d4de175db4ab73e7f4a02ed71cb777409584923158bd6e49d17c3c9ff940998&req=dSUjE812noJdUfMW1HO4za0dBweOypgqQuNDMH0dXKBq%2BxYbCy7eWewgjIlh%0AJVMexCRTcTY%2BWpHuBTc%3D%0A)

- Log in to your **Partner Dashboard**.

- Navigate to **My Themes → Themes Management**.

- Select your theme and click **Submit to Publish**.

- Confirm that all details, images, and documentation are accurate and complete.

- Submit your theme for Zid’s review team to evaluate.

# Theme Review Statuses

**Status**

**Description**

**Pending**

Your theme is awaiting review by the Zid team.

**In Review**

Your theme is currently under evaluation.

**Rejected**

Your theme requires changes; you will receive feedback with reasons.

**Published**

Your theme has been approved and is live on the Zid Theme Market.

📩 During the review process, Zid may contact you via your registered email address. To ensure you receive all communications, please add ** [email protected] ** to your email provider’s safe sender list.
