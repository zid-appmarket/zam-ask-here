> The article explains how to register a partner account and utilize the partner dashboard for creating and managing applications & Themes.
## **Partner Dashboard**

To start building awesome applications or themes and consume Zid's expressive APIs, you need to have an account on our partner dashboard.

Through the Partner Dashboard, you can do the following:

- Submit partnership details to receive your agreement to publish your App in our marketplace.

- Build and create your applications and themes, update, monitor installations, and subscriptions.

- Create a development store so you can test your App until you are ready to publish it on the market.

- Submit your App to be reviewed and listed on the market.

You can register your account in a few easy guided steps, and you can track the progress of your account completion from the dashboard.

## **Create A New Partner Account**

- Go to the developers' site here (https://partners.zid.sa)

- Click on Sign Up.

- Fill in the needed fields

- **Full Name** "Write your Company name" , "this will appear as App developer name"

- **E-mail address** "Use an email didn’t use before to create a Zid store"

- **Mobile Number**: Must be GCC number

- **Password**: 8 characters at least and contains numbers and letters

- **Country**:

- **Are you**?:

- An employee in a company

- Independent Developer "Freelancer"

- **What Is Your Application (Plugin) for**?

- **Public App**: "can be used by all stores in Zid"

- **Private App:** "can be used by specific stores in Zid"

- **Are You Looking For Ideas To Build?**: "We may suggest to you some needed ideas to build your Apps"
