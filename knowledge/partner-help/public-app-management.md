# **Managing App Reviews and Uninstall Reasons**

- **Login to Partner Dashboard**

The partner logs into the Zid Partner Dashboard.

- **Navigate to My Apps**

From the sidebar, go to **"My Apps"**.

A list of the partner’s published applications will be displayed.

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/1635819528/07d9d973b83612ee39e92f965910/My+Apps.png?expires=1794345300&signature=4e89d12f428128be70fa2004931588ac2b947bb145f85974a6f933b3bef819c5&req=dSYkE8F%2FlIRdUfMW1HO4zZwVlQHwQGjtS1dxJBOt1xGVzAEatozGKuGPoeGg%0ASsao%0A)

Click on the **"View"** button under the **Merchant Reviews** column for the desired app.

On the next screen, two tabs will be available:

- **Rates & Reviews**

## Subscription Statuses:

- ACTIVE: The subscription status would be ACTIVE for T-3 days where T is the duration of the plan in days.

- WARNING: The subscription status is set to WARNING for the last 3 days. This indicates the merchant need to renew the plan before it is moved to SUSPEND.

- SUSPEND: The subscription status would be SUSPEND when the duration of the plan is finished. This indicates the application is installed with a GRACE PERIOD of T+5 days.

- EXPIRED: The subscription status would be EXPIRED at T+5 days where T is tne duration of the plan in days. This indicates the application is uninstalled from the store

- RENEW: The window will open for merchant to re-purchase the same plan again from T-5 days where T is tne duration of the plan in days.

- UPGRADE: The plan can be upgraded at any point of time to any of the available plans.

- REFUND: The subscription can be REFUNDED with the given time as per the ZID refund policy
