# Theme Development Roadmap

To effectively start your journey in theme creation, follow these guided steps and explore each linked article:

## **1. Theme Type and Appearance (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/11471758-theme-type-and-appearance)**

Understand the two types of themes supported in Zid: Public Themes and Private Themes.

## **2. Create & Update Your Public Theme (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/11471777-create-update-your-public-theme)**

Learn how to create, upload, edit, and submit public themes for review and publication.

## **3. Create a Private Theme (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/11471800-create-a-private-theme)**

Explore how to develop, test, and deliver private themes directly to merchants.

## 4. Theme Store Visibility (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/17243601-improve-your-theme-visibility-in-the-zid-theme-store)

Learn how Industries, preset category tags, pricing, filters, sorting, and Featured/Recommended placement affect how merchants discover your theme.

## **5. Test Your Theme (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/11461482-test-your-theme)**

Detailed instructions for testing both public and private themes before going live.

## **6. Publish Your Theme (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/11471817-publish-your-theme)**

Follow the final steps for submitting your theme for review, including a pre-submission checklist.

## **7. Theme Analytics (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/8684798-theme-analytics)**

Track key performance metrics such as sales, activations, and merchant engagement.

## **8. Maintain Your Theme Support (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/11471850-maintain-your-theme-support)**

Best practices and requirements for providing timely and effective theme support.

## **9. Theme Payouts (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/17432699-theme-payouts)**

Understand the payout process, **automatic invoice generation**, payment statuses, and tax obligations for Theme Partners.

**Technical Prerequisites**: Ensure Node.js and NPM (version 1.1.0 or higher) are installed on your machine to support the theme development process.

By following this guide, you'll be equipped to craft a storefront that not only looks great but also provides a seamless shopping experience for your customers. Enjoy the process of bringing your vision to life with Zid Themes.
