> This article explains how Landing Page Partner payouts, automatic invoice generation, tax handling, and invoice corrections work.
Once your landing page is published and merchants begin purchasing or installing it, if paid, you become eligible for monthly payouts. Zid follows a structured payout process to ensure transparent invoice generation, review, and settlement.

The payout and invoice flow depends on the **tax type configured in the approved agreement for each landing page**.

## Payout Process Overview

For landing pages covered by an approved agreement with the **VAT** tax type, Zid automatically generates the VAT invoice for each eligible landing page and full calendar month.

You do **not** need to manually upload an invoice when the VAT payout is first created.

For VAT payouts:

- The regular invoice generation run takes place on the **1st of each month** for the month that just ended.

- An additional run takes place on the **15th** to catch eligible invoices from earlier months that were missed during the regular run.

- Once the invoice is generated, the payout appears with the **Under review** status.

- The invoice is available from the payout's **Attachments** section.

- Partners are **not notified when the automatic invoice is generated**, so you should check the Payouts page for new invoices.

Landing pages that are not covered by the VAT automatic invoice flow follow a separate automatic process on the **6th of the month** for the previous month.

## Automatic VAT Invoice Generation

For landing pages whose approved agreement uses the **VAT** tax type, Zid generates the VAT invoice automatically.

Each invoice covers:

- **One landing page**

- **One full calendar month**

If you have multiple eligible landing pages, a separate invoice is generated for each one.

For example, if you have three VAT-eligible landing pages, three separate invoices are generated for that month.

## Invoice Generation Schedule

### 1st of the month

- The regular run generates invoices for the **full calendar month that just ended**.

- **Example: **An invoice generated on **1 October** covers **September**.

### 15th of the month

- The 15th is a **catch-up run**.

- It does **not** cover the first half of the current month.

- Instead, it generates eligible invoices from previous months that were not generated during the regular 1st-of-the-month run.

- For example, this may happen when a VAT agreement is approved after the 1st.

- In many cases, no additional invoice may be generated on the 15th.

### Generation Time

- The automated process runs at **03:30 Riyadh time**.

- If the scheduled date falls on a **Friday, Saturday, or national holiday**, invoice generation moves to the **next working day**.

- The invoice date is the date on which the invoice is generated.

To access an automatically generated invoice:

- Go to **Payments → Payouts** in the Partner Dashboard.

- Select the **Templates** tab.

- Find the relevant month's payout.

- Filter by **Month**, or

- Search using the **payout code**.

- Click **Open details**.

- Open **Attachments**.

- Select **Invoice.pdf** to preview or download the invoice.

📜 **Important:**

- Once the invoice is generated, the payout appears with the **Under review** status.

- The manual invoice upload box is no longer displayed after the automatic VAT invoice is generated.

- Partners are **not notified by email or dashboard notification when the automatic invoice is generated**. Check the **Payments → Payouts** page to view the latest payout and invoice.

## Payout Lifecycle & Invoice Statuses

To check and manage your payouts:

- Navigate to **Payments → Payouts → Templates**.

- Use **Download Report** to review detailed transaction data.

- Open **Payout Details** to view invoice attachments, payment information, or upload a corrected invoice when required.

**Status**

**Description**

**Pending**

A payout that still requires an action before Finance review. This may apply to payout flows that do not use automatic VAT invoice generation.

**Under review / In Review**

Zid Finance is reviewing the payout and invoice. Automatically generated VAT payouts move directly to this stage.

**Rejected**

**Settled**

The payout has been approved and payment has been processed. You can access the receipt from the payout details.

## VAT Payout Flow

For landing pages with an approved VAT agreement:

- **Invoice generated automatically**

- Zid generates the VAT invoice for the eligible landing page.

- No manual invoice upload is required at this stage.

- **Under review**

- The payout moves to **Under review**.

- Zid Finance reviews the invoice and payout details.

- **Rejected, if applicable**

- If Finance identifies an issue, the payout is rejected.

- A note explaining the rejection reason is provided.

- You receive an email about the rejection.

- You can upload a corrected invoice.

- **Settled**

- Once the invoice and payout are approved, Zid processes the payment.

- The payout status changes to **Settled**.

## Non-VAT Payout Flow

Landing pages that are not covered by the VAT automatic invoice flow use a separate automatic process.

- The flow runs on the **6th of each month** for the previous month.

- The applicable tax treatment is based on the approved agreement.

- This includes cases where no VAT applies or withholding tax is applicable.

## Missing or Incorrect Invoices

If you cannot find an expected invoice or notice incorrect information:

- Go to **Partner Dashboard → Help & Support → Contact Us**.

- Contact the support team through the available support chat.

- Include the **payout code** so the affected payout can be identified.

### If an Invoice Is Missing

Common reasons include:

- The landing page is not covered by an approved **VAT** agreement and follows the separate 6th-of-the-month flow.

- The VAT agreement has a **0% rate**.

- The relevant calendar month has not ended yet.

- The payout has already been uploaded, rejected, or settled.

### If an Invoice Is Incorrect

If Finance identifies an issue with the generated invoice:

- Finance rejects the payout and adds a note explaining the issue.

- You receive an email about the rejection.

- Review the rejection reason in the Partner Dashboard.

- Upload a corrected invoice.

The automatically generated invoice is not regenerated through the normal partner flow. If regeneration is required, it must be handled by the Zid team.
