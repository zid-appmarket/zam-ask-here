## How Your Subscription Plans Appear in Zid App Market

Subscription plans are displayed as **comparison cards** directly on the app details page in both the merchant dashboard and public Zid App Market.

Each plan card displays:

- Plan name

- Subscription price

- Billing period

- Included features

Plans are displayed from the lowest price to the highest price.

### **Monthly/Yearly Toggle**

When an application offers both monthly and yearly subscription plans, merchants can switch between them using a **Monthly/Yearly toggle**.

A **"Save X%"** label highlights the savings calculated using the application's cheapest monthly and yearly plans.

### **Price Sorting**

When merchants sort applications by price in the Zid App Market, the system uses each application's **cheapest subscription plan** to determine its position. App cards also display full subscription prices and billing periods, helping merchants compare pricing before opening an app's details page.

**Note:** Existing plans automatically appear in the updated layout. Partners are encouraged to review their plan names, pricing, and feature descriptions for accuracy.

## **App Add-ons**

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/2800401816/78f37d1b0c99ab75086c1bdc922c/image.png?expires=1794346200&signature=4ce351249d7e4506e951abe68a418bd157eb7d8ef61cfeaedc3d8c2f5719c1ce&req=dignFs1%2BnIleX%2FMW1HO4zXMQi0C9Zh3C9JG%2BEM9vdnRhzGFJT4IG5NrcOWjk%0AlRGavrcdULGyaRP1WtA%3D%0A)

**App Add-ons** allow you to sell additional features or services separately from your application's subscription plans. Merchants can purchase add-ons through Zid's payment flow without changing their existing subscription plan.

### Types of Add-ons

**Type**

**Description**

**One-time**

A single payment for an additional feature or service, such as setup assistance or extra message credits.

**Monthly**

A recurring payment that renews automatically each month until cancelled.

### **Add-on Visibility**

You can choose who can purchase your add-ons:

- **Public Add-ons:** Available to all merchants who have installed your application. Public add-ons require Zid's approval before publication.

- **Targeted Add-ons:** Offered to a specific merchant using their Store ID. The merchant must already have your application installed. Targeted add-ons do not require review and become active immediately.

### **How to Create an Add-on**

- Sign in to your Partner Dashboard (https://partners.zid.sa/).

- Navigate to **Apps** and select your application.

- Open the **Add-ons** tab.

- Enter the add-on details:

- **Name:** Add the name in Arabic and English.

- **Description:** Describe the add-on in both languages.

- **Type:** Select One-time or Monthly.

- **Price:** Set the add-on price.

- **Unit and Quantity (Optional):** Specify what the merchant receives, such as 500 messages.

- **Visibility:** Select Public or Targeted. For targeted add-ons, enter the merchant's Store ID.

- Save your add-on and submit it for review if it is public.

### **Managing Add-ons**

**Public Add-ons**

Public add-ons follow this lifecycle:

`Draft → Review → Published → Archived`

- New public add-ons require Zid's approval.

- Editing a published add-on sends it back for review.

- Archived add-ons are no longer available for new purchases. Existing monthly subscribers retain access until they cancel.

**Targeted Add-ons**

- Are available only to the specified merchant.

- Do not require Zid's review.

- Expire after 30 days if the merchant does not purchase them.

### Monthly Add-on Billing

- Monthly add-ons renew based on the original purchase date.

- Merchants receive a renewal reminder three days before the payment date.

- If renewal payment fails, the add-on remains accessible during a **5-day grace period** while payment is retried.

- When a merchant cancels, access remains available until the end of the paid period.

- If the app subscription is suspended, automatic add-on renewals are paused until the subscription resumes.

### Add-on Sales and Revenue

You can track add-on purchases in the **Add-on Sales** list, including the purchasing store, add-on type, amount, payment status, and billing period.

Add-on revenue is included in your monthly payouts and follows the same revenue-sharing rules as your application plans.

**Note:** Add-on purchases, renewals, cancellations, and refunds can be handled through App Market webhooks and the Add-on Purchases API to automate service fulfillment.

## **Which Billing Option Should You Use?**

Zid offers different billing options depending on how you want to charge merchants for your application's services.

**Billing Option**

**Best Used For**

**Subscription Plan**

Offering your application's core features through recurring subscriptions.

**Private Plan**

Providing customized subscription pricing or features to a specific merchant.

**Public Add-on**

Selling optional features or services to all merchants who have installed your app.

**Targeted Add-on**

Offering a one-time or monthly service to a specific merchant using their Store ID.

**Usage-Based Plan**

Charging merchants based on their actual usage, such as messages, orders, or API calls.

**Tip:** Use subscription plans for your application's core services and add-ons for additional features or services that merchants can purchase separately.
