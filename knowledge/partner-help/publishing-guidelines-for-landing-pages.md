> This guide will help you understand the necessary steps and requirements to follow before publishing your landing page.
# **Accessing the Landing Pages Dashboard**

- **Login to Zid App Marke**:

- Visit Zid Partner Dashboard (https://partner.zid.sa/) and log in with your credentials (email and password).

- **Navigate to the Landing Pages Section**:

- On the left sidebar, click on the **My Theme** menu to expand it.

- Select **Landing Pages**. This will direct you to the landing page dashboard where you can manage your created landing pages.

# **Pre-Publishing Checklist**

Before you publish your landing page, make sure you have completed the following tasks:

## **1. Complete Landing Page Details**

- **Landing Page Name**: Ensure your landing page has a descriptive and clear name.

- **Categories**: Ensure the landing page is categorized correctly. You can select up to five categories that best represent your landing page.

- **Price**: Double-check the final price merchants will pay. If applicable, also provide a discounted price.

- **Description**: Provide a short and clear description of the landing page features in both **English** and **Arabic**.

- **Images**: Make sure to upload the correct number of images (between 3 and 9). Ensure the image quality is high and follows the required size ratios:

- **Desktop image**: ratio of 4:3, max size **1600w x 1200h**, min size **800w x 600h**.

- **Mobile image**: ratio of 56.6:100, max size **720w x 1280h**, min size **360w x 640h**.

## **2. Technical Support**

- Ensure that you have provided a **technical support email** to assist merchants in case of issues.

## **3. Preview the Landing Page**

- Always preview your landing page before submitting it. Ensure that all links, buttons, and content appear as intended.

- Test both **desktop** and **mobile** views.

## **4. Confirm Template Upload**

- If you are uploading a ZIP file containing the landing page, ensure that it’s the correct file format and all necessary files are included.

- If you’re using the **template details** option, make sure all fields (price, categories, URL, etc.) are completed.

# **Steps to Publish Your Landing Page**

Once you are confident that your landing page is ready and meets all necessary requirements, follow these steps to publish it:

## **1. Navigate to the Landing Pages Dashboard**

- On the left sidebar, click **My Theme** > **Landing Pages** to see all your landing pages.

## **2. Open the Landing Page**

- Click on the **Landing Page Name** that you want to publish. This will open the detailed view where you can make final changes.

## **3. Review the Status**

- In the **Status** column, ensure that the landing page is in **Draft** mode. If it’s ready for publishing, change the status to **Published**.

## **4. Click on Publish**

- There will be a **Publish** toggle button next to the landing page name. Switch it to **On** to make the page live.

## **5. Confirmation**:

- Once published, the landing page will be available to all merchants. The **Status** will change to **Published** and will be visible in the **Landing Pages** section of the Zid App Market.

# **Post-Publishing Considerations**

## **1. Monitoring Landing Page Performance**

- After publishing, it’s essential to monitor the performance of your landing page. Check how merchants are engaging with it by reviewing metrics available in the **Themes Analytics** section.

## **2. Make Changes After Publishing**

- If you need to make changes after publishing your landing page, you can edit the page by going back to the **Landing Pages** section and clicking the **Edit** icon. Make the necessary changes and save them.

## **3. Unpublishing**

- If you want to unpublish the landing page, simply turn off the **Publish** toggle to **Off**.

# **Guidelines for Quality and Compliance**

Before publishing your landing page, ensure the following:

- **Clear Messaging**: Your landing page should clearly communicate the product or service being offered. Avoid clutter and focus on a concise, attractive presentation.

- **Mobile-Optimized**: Ensure that the page is optimized for both mobile and desktop use. Most merchants will access the page from various devices.

- **Professional Quality**: Ensure that the design and content maintain a high level of professionalism. Poorly designed or low-quality content could reflect negatively on your app.

- **Compliance with Zid Policies**: All landing pages must comply with Zid’s terms and conditions. Make sure that your page adheres to all applicable rules, including but not limited to, privacy regulations and content restrictions.

# **Conclusion**

By following these publishing guidelines, you ensure that your landing page meets Zid's standards for quality, functionality, and merchant satisfaction. Proper publishing ensures that your landing page is ready for merchants to use and that it will function smoothly on the Zid App Market.
