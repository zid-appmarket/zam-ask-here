> A complete guide to navigating your Zid Partner Dashboard, managing apps and themes, tracking performance and payments, and accessing your partner tools.
The **Zid Partner Dashboard** is your central workspace for managing your partnership with Zid. Whether you're developing applications, publishing themes, monitoring subscriptions, tracking payments, or managing your team, the dashboard brings together the tools you need in one place.

This guide walks you through the dashboard's main sections, explains what you can do in each area, and directs you to detailed guides when you need help completing a specific task.

By the end of this guide, you'll know how to navigate your dashboard and find the tools and information relevant to your work as a Zid Partner.

## **1. Getting Started with Your Partner Dashboard**

### Access your dashboard

- Visit the Zid Partner Dashboard (https://partners.zid.sa/).

- Sign in using your registered partner account credentials.

- After signing in, you'll be directed to your main Dashboard page.

If you haven't registered as a Zid Partner, follow the Create Your Partner Account (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/8635315-create-your-partner-account) guide to get started.

### Understanding the dashboard layout

The dashboard is organized into three main areas:

**Area**

**Description**

Navigation sidebar

Provides access to the main dashboard sections, including Apps, Themes, Payments, and other partner tools.

Main workspace

Displays information and actions related to the selected section.

Top navigation bar

Provides access to account-related controls, notifications, language preferences, and other available options.

The left sidebar is your primary navigation tool. Some sections contain expandable menus that provide access to additional pages and workflows.

**Note:** The sections and actions available to you may depend on your partner account, enabled services, and team member permissions.

### Key performance metrics

At the top of the page, you'll find summary cards highlighting important information about your partner account.

**Metric**

**Description**

Total Income

Displays the income recorded for the selected reporting context.

Total Subscriptions

The **Subscription Analytics** section helps you understand subscription activity over time.

You can switch between the available views:

- **Trial & Paid:** Displays the combined subscription activity.

- **Trial:** Focuses on trial subscriptions.

- **Paid:** Focuses on paid subscriptions.

### Installation Analytics

The **Installation Analytics** section provides visibility into installation activity for your applications.

This information helps you:

- Monitor installation trends.

- Identify periods of increased or decreased activity.

- Understand how installation activity changes over time.

- Evaluate application adoption alongside subscription performance.

### Latest Installs

The **Latest Installs** section displays recent installation activity.

The table may include information such as:

**Field**

**Description**

Store Name

The merchant store associated with the installation.

Plan

The subscription plan associated with the installation.

Install Date

The date the application was installed.

This section is useful for monitoring recent adoption without opening the full subscriptions list.

### Latest Ratings & Reviews

This section provides a quick view of recent ratings and reviews associated with your offerings.

Regularly reviewing merchant feedback can help you identify opportunities to improve the quality and experience of your applications.

### Filter dashboard data

At the top of the Dashboard page, you can use the available selection and date-range controls to change the data displayed.

To review a specific reporting period:

- Open the **Dashboard** page.

- Use the available dropdown to select the relevant reporting context.

- Open the date-range selector.

- Choose the desired period.

- Review the updated metrics and analytics.

**Tip:** If a chart displays **"No data available for the selected period,"** try selecting a different date range or reporting context. This message means no data is available for the current selection.

## **3. Apps**

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/2765180145/5505a29fda5e9521e1a6df6bc278/image.png?expires=1794346200&signature=e56b6241d0d8718f4bd83c6998e4019cec00b4e6e81a20da89f0349fd5f1ac49&req=dichE8h2nYBbXPMW1HO4zZ5MyIbvwS0ede3Dbp15d8EWBRd4HWFEes1gDTCg%0AolYkBnj5MH8yCKTwe1A%3D%0A)

**Navigation:** `Partner Dashboard → Apps`

The **Apps** section is where you create, manage, and monitor applications developed for Zid merchants.

It contains two main pages:

- All Apps

- Subscriptions

### 3.1 All Apps

**Navigation:** `Apps → All Apps`

The **All Apps** page provides access to the applications associated with your partner account.

From this page, you can manage the application lifecycle, including creating applications, reviewing existing app information, and accessing available management actions.

Depending on the application and its status, you can use this area to:

- Create a new application.

- Review existing applications.

- Check application status.

- Access application details.

- Update application information and configurations.

- Review installation-related information.

- Manage available application settings.

#### Creating a new application

To start creating an application:

- Open **Apps → All Apps**.

- Select the available application creation action.

- Choose the appropriate application type.

- Complete the required application information.

- Follow the relevant application creation and submission process.

The available steps depend on whether you're developing a public or private application.

**Related guides:**

- Create a Public App (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/8637655-create-a-public-app)

- Create a Private App (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/17348249-create-a-private-app)

- Integration and Development Cycle (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/8716995-integration-and-development-cycle)

### 3.2 Subscriptions

**Navigation:** `Apps → Subscriptions`

The **Subscriptions** page allows you to monitor merchant subscriptions to your applications.

Use it to review subscription activity, track subscription status, and find information about stores that have subscribed to your apps.

#### Search and filter subscriptions

The subscription list provides controls for locating specific records.

**Filter or control**

**Purpose**

Search

Find a subscription using a merchant store name or Store ID.

Select App

Display subscriptions associated with a specific application.

Subscription Status

Filter subscriptions according to their current status.

Date filters

Narrow results to a selected subscription period.

Pagination

Control the number of records displayed per page.

Export All

Export subscription records when the option is available.

### Understanding subscription information

Subscription records can include:

**Field**

**Description**

Store Name / ID

Identifies the merchant store associated with the subscription.

Plan Name

The subscription plan selected by the merchant.

Amount Paid

The amount recorded for the subscription.

Plan Type

Indicates whether the subscription is free or paid.

Subscription Status

Shows the current subscription state, such as active or expired.

Start / End Date

Indicates the subscription validity period.

Installation Status

Shows whether the application has been activated or installed.

Contact Information

Provides the merchant store's available contact details.

Recurring

Indicates whether automatic subscription renewal is enabled.

**Important:** A merchant's subscription status and application installation status are different. A merchant may have a subscription without having completed the application activation process.

### How to find a merchant subscription

- Navigate to **Apps → Subscriptions**.

- Select the relevant application if needed.

- Search using the store name or Store ID.

- Apply the appropriate status or date filters.

- Review the matching subscription record.

This is particularly useful when investigating installation issues, subscription questions, or merchant support requests.

## **6. Coupons**

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/2765187397/83e1439e78380e2c7b59db472e71/image.png?expires=1794346200&signature=9c241958cce6581c404a995d07336875bbb57e185f9b24e2692cf0c81a2aa6e3&req=dichE8h2moJWXvMW1HO4zeFBTX4Jzp3AxBnly50wOY4bCW7Nz%2B%2F6tunDwNTy%0AIEmbW3itv%2BN5HNWQix8%3D%0A)

**Navigation:** `Partner Dashboard → Coupons`

The **Coupons** section allows partners to manage promotional discount codes for eligible app or theme purchases.

Coupons can help support promotional campaigns and encourage merchant adoption.

### Manage your coupons

From the Coupons page, you can review existing promotional codes and access the available coupon management actions.

Coupon information can include:

- Coupon code.

- Current status.

- Discount percentage.

- Start and expiration dates.

- Redemption or usage information.

### Create a coupon

To create a promotional coupon:

- Open **Coupons** from the navigation sidebar.

- Select the available coupon creation action.

- Enter the required coupon details.

- Configure the applicable discount conditions and usage restrictions.

- Save the coupon.

### Understanding coupon settings

**Setting**

**Description**

Coupon Code

The promotional code merchants enter when redeeming the discount.

Status

Determines whether the coupon is active or inactive.

Start / End Date

Defines when the coupon can be used.

Discount Percentage

The percentage discount applied to eligible purchases.

Minimum Value

The minimum purchase value required to use the coupon.

Applicable Selections

Determines which eligible offerings the coupon applies to.

Targeted Stores

Restricts usage to specified merchant Store IDs when configured.

Usage Limit

Controls whether the coupon has limited or unlimited redemptions.

Total Number of Coupons

Sets the overall redemption limit when limited usage is selected.

Number of Uses Per Store

Restricts how many times an individual merchant store can use the coupon.

### Understanding usage limits

- **Unlimited:** The coupon can be redeemed without a predefined total redemption limit, subject to its other eligibility conditions and validity period.

- **Limited:** The coupon can only be redeemed up to the configured maximum number of times.

You can also restrict usage at the individual store level when supported.

**Important:** According to Zid's coupon payout rules, coupon discounts are excluded from Zid's share in payout calculations.

**Tip:** Before making a promotional code available to merchants, review its eligibility conditions, date range, and usage limits to ensure the campaign works as intended.

## **7. Development Store**

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/2765216353/62a700f75aeed0418fe00e0dfe0a/image.png?expires=1794346200&signature=fe6510f1314607a7d2f13b0a7210e72b27ce8177bc81aa3c25de3d4e68d228a5&req=dichE8t%2Fm4JaWvMW1HO4zbzeJkEq5LwQcFZ%2BJmlZJ9YCyyRyx%2Fa%2BKqvblTvZ%0A%2FrbsNaEeEJ5BbiO5Vj0%3D%0A)

**Navigation:** `Partner Dashboard → Development Store`

A **Development Store** provides a merchant-like environment where partners can test their applications and themes before releasing them to merchants.

It is especially useful for validating integration behavior, interface changes, and important merchant workflows.

### What can you use a development store for?

You can use development stores to:

- Test application installation and functionality.

- Validate interactions between apps and the merchant dashboard.

- Test API integrations in a development environment.

- Preview and test theme behavior.

- Verify changes before submitting an app or theme for review.

- Identify issues before merchant adoption.

### Create a development store

- Sign in to your Partner Dashboard.

- Navigate to **Development Store**.

- Select the available store creation action.

- Complete any required information.

- Open the newly created store from your development store list.

Once the store is available, use the provided access information and store entry to enter its merchant environment.

### Testing your application or theme

After accessing the development store:

- Open the development store's merchant interface.

- Install or enable the application or theme you're testing, where applicable.

- Complete the relevant merchant workflows.

- Verify expected functionality and integration behavior.

- Resolve any issues before submitting the final version.

**Tip:** Use development stores to test important scenarios such as installation, authentication, configuration, storefront display, and checkout-related behavior, depending on your application or theme.

For more guidance on development and publishing, see Integration and Development Cycle (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/8716995-integration-and-development-cycle).

### Monitor webhook activity

Use the available webhook pages and logs to investigate event delivery and integration behavior.

Webhook information can include:

**Field**

**Description**

Store ID

Event

The webhook event that occurred.

The payout or related documentation is undergoing review.

Rejected

The payout or associated submission requires attention.

Settled

The payout has completed the applicable settlement process.

The available statuses and required actions depend on the agreement and payout type.

### 12.3 View and download invoices

For services covered by an approved VAT agreement, Zid automatically generates applicable invoices.

To access a generated invoice:

- Navigate to **Payments → Payouts**.

- Select **App subscriptions**, **Themes**, or **Templates**.

- Find the relevant payout record.

- Select **Open details**.

- Open the **Attachments** section.

- Select **Invoice.pdf** to view or download the invoice.

**Important:** Automatically generated invoices may not be available immediately when a payout record first appears. Invoice handling depends on the approved agreement and applicable generation process.

For complete details about invoices, payout schedules, tax handling, and settlement, see Get Started with Partner Payouts (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/8750045-get-started-with-partner-payouts).

The **Bank Account** page provides access to banking information associated with your partner account.

Use this section to review or manage available bank account details as permitted by your account.

**Tip:** Keep your payment and banking information accurate to help prevent payout processing issues.

## **16. Frequently Asked Questions**

### What's the difference between the main Dashboard and the Theme Dashboard?

The main **Dashboard** provides an overview of your partner account, including income, subscription activity, installation analytics, and recent activity.

The **Themes → Dashboard** page focuses specifically on theme-related performance, including activations, purchases, and previews.

### Where can I create a new app?

Navigate to **Apps → All Apps** and select the available application creation action.

For step-by-step instructions, see Create a Public App (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/8637655-create-a-public-app).

### Where can I manage my themes?

Navigate to **Themes → All Themes** to access theme listings and management options.

### How can I check merchant subscriptions?

Go to **Apps → Subscriptions**. Search for the relevant merchant or filter the list by application, status, or date.

### Where can I find my earnings and invoices?

Navigate to **Payments → Payouts**. Select the applicable payout type and open the relevant payout record.

Generated invoices are available under the payout's **Attachments** section when applicable.

### Why can't I see a particular dashboard section?

Some dashboard pages and actions depend on your account permissions and available services.

If you're a team member, contact your account administrator to verify that you've been granted the required access.

### Why does my dashboard show no analytics data?

You may see this message when there is no activity recorded for the selected reporting context or date range.

Try adjusting the reporting period or selection. If you expect data to be available but it remains missing, contact support.

### Where can I test my application before publishing?

Use the **Development Store** section to access a merchant-like environment for testing your application or theme.

### How can I give someone access to my Partner Dashboard?

Go to **Team Management** and use the available invitation or member creation action. Assign the appropriate role and permissions.

### Where can I find webhook logs?

Navigate to **Webhooks** and use the available application and date filters to locate relevant event records.

### Where can I manage my AI assistant connections?

Open **AI Connections** to access your partner MCP connection information and available connection management tools.

### Where can I get help?

Open **Help & Support** in the Partner Dashboard, or visit the Zid Partners Help Center (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/).
