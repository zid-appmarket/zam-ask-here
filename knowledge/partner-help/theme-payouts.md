> This article explains how Theme Partner payouts, automatic invoice generation, tax handling, payout statuses, and invoice corrections work in the Partner Dashboard.
As a Theme Partner, once your theme is published and merchants begin purchasing or installing it, if paid, you are eligible for monthly payouts. Zid follows a structured payment process to ensure transparent invoice generation, review, and settlement.

The invoice flow depends on the **tax type configured in the approved agreement for each theme**.

## Payout Process Overview

For themes covered by an approved agreement with the **VAT** tax type, Zid automatically generates the VAT invoice for each eligible theme and full calendar month.

You do **not** need to manually upload an invoice when the VAT payout is first created.

For VAT payouts:

- The regular invoice generation run takes place on the **1st of each month** for the month that just ended.

- An additional run takes place on the **15th** to catch eligible invoices from earlier months that were missed during the regular run.

- Once the invoice is generated, the payout appears with the **Under review** status.

- The invoice is available from the payout's **Attachments** section.

- Partners are not notified when the automatic invoice is generated, so you should check the Payouts page for new invoices.

Partners without a VAT agreement follow a separate automatic flow on the **6th of the month** for the previous month.

## Automatic VAT Invoice Generation

For themes whose approved agreement has the **VAT** tax type, Zid generates the VAT invoice automatically.

Each invoice covers:

- **One theme**

- **One full calendar month**

If you have multiple eligible themes, a separate invoice is generated for each theme.

For example, if you have three VAT-eligible themes, you receive three separate invoices for that month.

## Invoice generation schedule

### 1st of the month

The regular run generates invoices for the **full calendar month that just ended**.

**Example:**

An invoice generated on **1 October** covers **September**.

### 15th of the month

The 15th is a **catch-up run**.

It does **not** cover the first half of the current month.

Instead, it generates eligible invoices from previous months that were not generated during the regular run.

For example, this can happen when a VAT agreement is approved after the 1st.

In many cases, no additional invoice may be generated on the 15th.

### Generation time

The automated process runs at **03:30 Riyadh time**.

If the scheduled date falls on a **Friday, Saturday, or national holiday**, invoice generation moves to the **next working day**.

The invoice date is the date on which the invoice is generated.

## View or Download Your Invoice

To access an automatically generated invoice:

- Navigate to **Payments → Payouts** in the Partner Dashboard.

- Select the **Themes** tab.

- Find the relevant month's payout.

- Filter by **Month**, or

- Search using the **payout code**.

- Click **Open details**.

## Payout Details and Reports

Each payout contains the payments associated with that theme and payout period.

You can review the detailed transactions by clicking the **Download Report** option.

## Payout Statuses

**Status**

**Description**

**Awaiting invoice**

The payout requires an invoice before Finance review. This status applies to payout flows outside automatic VAT invoice generation.

**Under review**

Zid Finance is reviewing the payout and invoice. VAT payouts move to this status after the invoice is generated automatically.

**Rejected**

Finance identified an issue. Review the rejection reason and upload a corrected invoice if required.

**Settled**

The payout has been approved and payment has been completed.

## Payout Lifecycle

### VAT Payout Flow

For themes with an approved VAT agreement:

- **Invoice generated automatically**

- Zid creates the VAT invoice for the eligible theme.

- No manual invoice upload is required at this stage.

- **Under review**

- The payout moves to **Under review** after invoice generation.

- Zid's Finance team reviews the invoice and payout details.

- **Rejected, if applicable**

- If Finance identifies an issue, the payout is rejected.

- A note explaining the rejection reason is provided.

- You receive an email about the rejection.

- You can upload a corrected invoice.

- **Settled**

- Once the invoice and payout are approved, Zid processes the payment.

- The payout status changes to **Settled**.

You can access payment information and receipts from the payout details at any time.Settled (https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/1542502786/9451a25916c2bf7f85dc5dedccc9/image.png?expires=1794474000&signature=6193713f917c4cd58442fc2cc6c6f3ce57dfc0f52f93d77433b46042635be883&req=dSUjFMx%2Bn4ZXX%2FMW3Hu4gYeadfNdlUVD0Bto9Dsc%2BufJsDegOg3jMREQsy89%0AVA%3D%3D%0A)

### Non-VAT Payout Flow

Themes that are not covered by the VAT automatic invoice flow use a separate automatic process.

- The flow runs on the **6th of each month** for the previous month.

- The applicable tax treatment is based on the approved agreement.

- This includes cases where no VAT applies or withholding tax is applicable.

## Important Dates

### VAT agreements

- **1st of each month:** Regular automatic invoice generation for the previous full calendar month.

- **15th of each month:** Catch-up run for eligible invoices from previous months that were missed on the regular run.

- **03:30 Riyadh time:** Scheduled invoice generation time.

- **Friday, Saturday, or national holiday:** Generation moves to the next working day.

### Non-VAT agreements

- **6th of each month:** Separate automatic flow for the previous month.

## Missing or Incorrect Invoices

If you cannot find an expected invoice or notice an issue with an invoice:

- Go to **Partner Dashboard → Help & Support → Contact Us**.

- Contact the support team through the available support chat.

- Include the **payout code** so the affected payout can be identified.

### If an Invoice Is Missing

Common reasons include:

- The theme is not covered by an approved VAT agreement and follows the separate 6th-of-the-month flow.

- The VAT agreement has a **0% rate**.

- The relevant calendar month has not ended yet.

- The payout has already been processed, rejected, or settled.

### If an Invoice Is Incorrect

**If Finance identifies an issue:**

- Finance rejects the payout and adds a note explaining the reason.

- You receive an email about the rejection.

- Review the rejection note in the Partner Dashboard.

- Upload a corrected invoice and resubmit it.

The automatically generated invoice is not regenerated through the normal partner flow. If regeneration is required, it must be handled by the Zid team.
