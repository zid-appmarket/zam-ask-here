> This guide walks Zid Partners through the complete process of creating, submitting, and managing storefront landing pages.
# Overview

Landing Pages allow partners to create custom pages that merchants can add to their storefronts. These pages can be used for seasonal campaigns, promotional content, product highlights, and more, offering enhanced flexibility and branding for Zid merchants.

Landing Pages are managed through the **Partner Dashboard (https://partner.zid.sa/)**, (https://partner.zid.sa/) under the **Themes Analytics (https://partner.zid.sa/themes-dashboard)** section, and follow a workflow similar to themes and apps: creation, management, testing (coming soon), publishing (coming soon), and maintenance (coming soon).

# Accessing Landing Pages

To access and manage your landing pages:

- Go to the Zid Partner Dashboard (https://partner.zid.sa/).

- Log in using your registered email and password.

- From the sidebar:

- Expand the **Themes Analytics** section.

- Click **My Themes**.

- Switch to the **Landing Pages** tab.

Here you can view, manage, and track all your landing page templates.

# Creating a New Landing Page

To create a new landing page template:

- Click the **Add** button on the top right.

- In the **Choose Template Type** dialog, select **Landing Page**.

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/1585721923/d20bc41d4699a3a0d4a401bf73aa/Screenshot%2B2025-06-24%2B125619.png?expires=1794345300&signature=44f5003915618747dd2dd24b830d7d2488e0c4397f3a1c508469e7f9bc0df0a7&req=dSUvE858nIhdWvMW1HO4zSMtezDDCYtkHV4Cq06zKc6E34RPJdgPJMhjZgle%0ABf%2Fdd5VzWV1NmigLfDw%3D%0A)

3. Enter your **landing page name** in both Arabic and English.

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/1585721921/02bb3281ba77e9172d0ea244487a/Screenshot%2B2025-06-24%2B125641.png?expires=1794345300&signature=8ffbb408d6c2fe6e567e411f3cce7f146d68bf9b63d879de195eb50c75794730&req=dSUvE858nIhdWPMW1HO4zREiDAO9SKd54O5SpPlGkaWH9BqXbtCTUZhdOU2f%0ANFtejIkCMcbkwOWy3uQ%3D%0A)

4. Click **Create** to proceed to the setup steps.

## Step 1: Upload Template File

You’ll be prompted to upload your landing page ZIP file:

- **Template file:** Upload the landing page ZIP package (required).

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/1585721924/58302fae07bfa4148ed75368733a/Screenshot%2B2025-06-24%2B130116.png?expires=1794345300&signature=b5ea4f67495cd57b5839f4a93c7a5b93dd4686fc5665e6407778a8aa5e6231a5&req=dSUvE858nIhdXfMW1HO4zaHpi0IKKsUd1Til7iqo0zHhDiuC4lnErGhZd6v%2B%0ACZfVwO3VUqJVJKSAqoE%3D%0A)

- Click **Next** to continue.

## Step 2: Landing Page Details

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/1585721928/b6c002b533cc5739e21304b57208/Screenshot%2B2025-06-24%2B130145.png?expires=1794345300&signature=ec70744c9a583d45ffed201c48b2e8f921d6abca62724169d4378ce1683157e6&req=dSUvE858nIhdUfMW1HO4zdv8uDQ8EVhgibueR7KNwbtdhNgaHcqe8K%2BXGKh3%0AEbJ5dunlpMd53ns%2FtFE%3D%0A)

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/1585721931/3d0b224c355b5ac58821cdb3dd79/Screenshot%2B2025-06-24%2B130243.png?expires=1794345300&signature=6a27835e66728b90312cc53612f8f0a376b484dfc52e8fd311a9a01ed051bda8&req=dSUvE858nIhcWPMW1HO4zYahoc8Wd2vXAOFlX9WHGkR34XUeBcMLb%2FrxRyCs%0A0eGJjmr5x1HzFueTCfU%3D%0A)

Fill in the required details:

- **Price**: Final price paid by merchants.

- **Discounted Price** (optional) .

- **Landing Page Categories**: Select up to 5.

- **Preview Store URL**: A live store demonstrating the landing page.

- **Demo Store URL**: Optional demo environment.

- **Description**:

- Enter a brief overview of the landing page features in both English and Arabic.

- Character limit: 280 for each language.

- **Technical Support Email**: Contact for merchant inquiries.

- **Company Website URL**: Optional.

Click **Next** to proceed.

## Step 3: Landing Page Images

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/1585721932/386fb925addf4f723dc06855742d/Screenshot%2B2025-06-24%2B130316.png?expires=1794345300&signature=490b47c7e837c4a7cfa6c3501ec9a7cee2d7e62a152cb2bf6a2bb96d3cd6b28d&req=dSUvE858nIhcW%2FMW1HO4zc4qF1GO3tkqPwOZjn9cFKLosIIecORi3FFIZ70%2B%0A26dVe9V%2FZbhWMftqsvw%3D%0A)

Upload your landing page images. Ensure your submission meets these criteria:

- Minimum **3**, Maximum **9** images.

- Accepted formats: JPG, PNG.

- **Desktop Screenshot**:

- Aspect ratio: 4:3

- Max size: 1600x1200px

- Min size: 800x600px

- **Mobile Screenshot**:

- Aspect ratio: 56.6:100

- Max size: 720x1280px

- Min size: 360x640px

- **Featured Image**: Highlight image for display.

## Saving or Publishing

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/1585721929/6138f12910f0963f4b07f97eb7c1/image%2B-2840-29.png?expires=1794345300&signature=97766e3865370faa18e8a9952ee2caad518455889cdde6a6338883249d7020ef&req=dSUvE858nIhdUPMW1HO4zQmUM5wwdce8sTqIiOUsm62uBHpVDOMP%2BMS1s1Mj%0ANEDu1QQjHC%2FyBYItMWk%3D%0A)

Once all three steps are completed:

- Click **Save Draft** to store the template without publishing.

- Click **Submit to Publish** to send the template for Zid review and listing.
