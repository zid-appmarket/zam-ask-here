> Learn how to review and accept Zid Partner Terms & Conditions before creating apps, themes, or landing pages.
Zid Partners must review and accept the current **Terms & Conditions** before creating apps, themes, or landing pages.

The acceptance process replaces the previous contract signing and approval requirements, making it easier to start developing and publishing on Zid.

This guide explains the requirements, how to accept the terms, who can accept them, and what to do if you're unable to proceed.

## Before You Begin

Before creating an app, theme, or landing page, make sure you have an active Zid Partner account and have completed the required bank and business details.

## Bank Details

- **IBAN:** Provide a valid IBAN, or a bank account number if your country doesn't use IBAN.

- **SWIFT Code:** Required for all bank accounts.

- **Routing Number:** Required for US bank accounts.

## Business Details

- **Companies:** Provide your Commercial Registration (CR) number and upload the CR document.

- **Saudi-banked companies:** Indicate whether you're VAT-registered. If yes, provide your VAT number.

- **Freelancers:** Provide a passport or national ID.

**Important:** These requirements apply to both new and existing partners. If your bank or business details are incomplete, you cannot create an app, theme, or landing page until you provide the missing information.

## How to Accept the Terms & Conditions

The acceptance process is the same in both the old and new Partner Dashboards.

- Sign in to your Partner Dashboard (https://partners.zid.sa).

- Complete your **bank and business details**, if you haven't already.

- Select the appropriate creation action:

- **Apps:** Navigate to **Apps → All Apps → Create application**.

- **Themes:** Click **Add theme**.

- **Landing Pages:** Click **Create Landing Page**.

- Review the Terms & Conditions document when prompted.

- Select the checkbox to confirm your acceptance.

- Click **Accept** to continue.

Once accepted, you can proceed with creating your app, theme, or landing page.

**Note:** Acceptance is recorded for the relevant document type and version at the partner account level and is recognized across both Partner Dashboards.

## Who Can Accept the Terms & Conditions?

Only authorized users can accept the Terms & Conditions on behalf of a partner account.

**User**

**Permission**

Account Owner

Can accept the Terms & Conditions.

Team Member

Must have the `manage.partner.partnership.details` permission.

If you don't have the required permission, contact your account owner or an authorized team member.

**Note:** Acceptance applies to the entire partner account, not individual users. Once the account owner or an authorized team member accepts the current Terms & Conditions, everyone on the team can proceed with the relevant creation process according to their assigned permissions, without accepting the same version again.

## Terms & Conditions by Partner Type

Different partner activities use the following Terms & Conditions:

**Partner Type**

**Terms & Conditions**

App Partners

App Terms & Conditions

Theme Partners

Theme Terms & Conditions

Landing Page Partners

Theme Terms & Conditions (separate acceptance)

**Important:** Landing pages use the same Terms & Conditions text as themes, but acceptance is recorded separately for each document type.

Even if you've already accepted the Theme Terms & Conditions, you'll be asked to accept them separately when creating your first landing page.

## What Happens If You Haven't Accepted?

If you haven't accepted the current Terms & Conditions, you won't be able to proceed with creating a new app, theme, or landing page.

To continue:

- Make sure your bank and business details are complete and valid.

- Start the relevant creation process.

- Review and accept the current Terms & Conditions when prompted.

**Important:** This restriction also applies to existing partner accounts. If you haven't completed your required bank or business information, you must provide the missing details before creating anything new.

There is **no fixed acceptance deadline**. However, acceptance of the current version is required before you can proceed with creation.

## Terms & Conditions Versions

Zid records acceptance separately for each document type and version at the partner account level.

If Zid publishes a new version of the Terms & Conditions, you'll be asked to review and accept it again before creating another app, theme, or landing page under the affected document type.

Once an authorized user accepts the new version, it applies to the entire partner account. Other team members don't need to accept the same version individually.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to sign or upload a partnership contract?**

No. The previous contract-signing process has been replaced with electronic Terms & Conditions acceptance.

**Can a team member accept the Terms & Conditions?**

Yes. A team member with the `manage.partner.partnership.details` permission can accept the terms on behalf of the partner account. Once accepted, other authorized team members don't need to accept the same version again.

**Can I accept the terms from either Partner Dashboard?**

Yes. The process is the same in both dashboards, and acceptance is recognized across them.

**Why am I being asked to accept the Terms & Conditions for landing pages when I've already accepted them for themes?**

Landing pages and themes use the same Terms & Conditions text, but acceptance is recorded separately. You'll need to accept the terms for landing pages when creating your first landing page, even if you've already accepted them for themes.

**Will I need to accept the Terms & Conditions again if Zid updates them?**

Yes. If Zid publishes a new version, an authorized user must accept it before the account can create new items under the affected document type.

**Why can't I create an app, theme, or landing page?**

Make sure your bank and business details are complete and valid, and that an authorized user has accepted the current Terms & Conditions for the relevant document type. These requirements apply to both new and existing partner accounts.
