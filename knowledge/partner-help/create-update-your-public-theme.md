> This article discusses the steps to create and update public themes on Zid, including uploading, editing, and submitting themes for review.
# 1. Steps to Create a New Public Theme

- Log in to the **Zid Partner Dashboard**

- Go to **My Themes → Themes Management**

- Click **Create New Theme**

- Fill in the theme name, upload the ZIP package

- Under **Theme Details**, define the theme's price, description, display information, **Industries, and Catalog sizes.**

**Industries** identify the types of stores your theme is best suited for and help determine where your theme appears when merchants filter the Theme Store by category. Select only industries that accurately match your theme. Learn more about how listing information affects discovery in **Improve Your Theme Visibility in the Zid Theme Store (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/17243601-improve-your-theme-visibility-in-the-zid-theme-store)**.

**Catalog Sizes**

Select the catalog sizes your theme is designed to support. This helps merchants find themes suitable for their store's number of products.

Available options:

- **One Product:** 1 product

- **Small:** 2–10 products

- **Medium:** 11–100 products

- **Large:** 101–500 products

- **Mega:** 500+ products

You can select multiple options if your theme supports more than one catalog size.

**Note:** Catalog sizes is a required field when submitting a new theme. Select only the sizes your theme is designed and tested for.

- In **Theme Images**, upload: use this guide (Figma (https://www.figma.com/design/R3Fg5JzYB9O1PeeAK5GQx0/Zid-themes-Mockups---Duplicate-it-then-start?node-id=0-1) & Video (https://www.veed.io/view/ec1302b1-33f7-4fbd-b9f5-5262c8f7f3ce?panel=share)) to help you with ready mockups

- 3–9 images

- Desktop (4:3) — Max: 1600×1200, Min: 800×600

- Mobile (9:16) — Max: 720×1280, Min: 360×640

- Accept **Zid Terms & Conditions**

- Click **Save Draft** or **Submit to Publish**

# **2. Steps to Update a** Public **Theme**

If you have a published theme and wish to update it, here's how you can edit or upgrade it to a newer version:

## **Edit Theme Details & Images**

You can modify theme details and images anytime through the partner dashboard. You will be notified upon successful reflection of your changes.

The **Detailed Description** fields (Arabic and English) support a rich-text (HTML) editor, allowing you to format text, add headings and lists, and insert links and images.

When updating your theme details, review the selected **Industries** and make sure they still accurately represent the types of stores your theme is designed for. Accurate listing information helps merchants discover your theme through Theme Store filters.

## Update Catalog Sizes

You can update the **Catalog sizes** of an existing theme to ensure merchants can find it using the Theme Store's catalog size filters.

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/2765324137/5a703daaa464e4b877efcae480b5/image.png?expires=1794345300&signature=374353dcda84356d70998b2ef78de070810af487745992ec592c06d169a3a39b&req=dichE8p8mYBcXvMW1HO4zcFIuU0FlRyTrUvZF%2FNdNd2mmembX9uTywIHfp%2F9%0A%2F4m8tb2JHNG0uZzROFc%3D%0A)

To update your theme's Catalog sizes:

- Log in to your Partner Dashboard (https://partners.zid.sa/).

- Navigate to **Themes → All Themes → Vitrin**.

- Select the theme you want to update.

- Open the **Info** tab.

- Under **Catalog sizes**, select all the product ranges your theme is designed and tested to support.

- Click **Save changes**.

- Review the confirmation message and click **Save anyway** to confirm.

**Note:** Themes without a selected Catalog Size are excluded from results when merchants apply Catalog Size filters in the Theme Store. Keep this information accurate to improve your theme's discoverability.

## **Upload a New Theme Version**

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/2753066781/282dc5f4fff0a6b9f222949fcebd/Screenshot+2026-10-20+at+3_04_27%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1794345300&signature=6446ee6af09b243e90c3f29a02228e08d1f9aa2516768b4bf2e318f4d682a2f0&req=diciFcl4m4ZXWPMW1HO4zdTyMds7SjvwkVVYk%2FjBfu2p1fW%2B47CkNbmUoj7T%0AKJKrtF1NPOp5bTFV2Zc%3D%0A)

- Log in to your Partner Dashboard (https://partner.zid.sa/).

- In the sidebar navigation, click **My Themes → Themes Management**.

- Click the **edit icon** next to the published theme, then go to the **Update Theme** tab.

- Choose the change type according to your theme modifications. Note that the version number will increase based on your input.

- Upload the updated theme file.

- Describe the changes made to the theme (e.g., new features, enhancements).

- Click **Save Draft** to save changes, or **Submit to Publish** to send the theme update for review.

## **Changing Your Theme Details**

You can modify your theme details and images as long as the theme is in Draft status or if it has been Rejected . Once the review process begins (status changed to In Review ), you cannot submit changes until the status updates to Published or Rejected . If changes are required during the review, please contact our support team.

# **Delete Your Theme**

Themes with Draft or Rejected status can be deleted to tidy up your theme list.

# Hide Your Theme from the Theme Store

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/2715573299/6fb2adcf9aa0433dd2019c57865c/image.png?expires=1794345300&signature=3e4f1c2f96bf25dfca26082959830f39ad3d0d1ee8e0519a553c517061b6a59c&req=dicmE8x5noNWUPMW1HO4zR9yeqxOF7FprsqJQQ9y4UUPjJ3Gxi919eZqUHpw%0AImKefjZfkLMwz84QuVw%3D%0A)

You can temporarily hide a published theme from the Theme Store without unpublishing or deleting it.

- Go to **Themes (https://partners.zid.sa/en-sa/partner/186/themes?tab=vitrin&page=1) → All Themes** in the Partner Dashboard (https://partners.zid.sa/ar-sa/partner/186).

- Find the published theme you want to hide.

- Click the **hide** icon next to the delete icon.

- In the confirmation message, click **Confirm**.

**When you hide a theme:**

- The theme remains published.

- It is no longer shown in the Theme Store to new merchants.

- Merchants who already purchased or activated the theme keep access.

- Their current theme continues to work normally.

You can make the theme visible in the Theme Store again at any time.

**Tip:** Use this option instead of deleting the theme when you only want to temporarily stop new merchants from accessing it.

# **Submit Your Theme for Review**

Our team reviews new themes for essential features, flexibility, resilience, and performance. The review process takes up to 2 days, and you can track the status through the partner dashboard.

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/u7vuis4c/1545454144/8c1fb15f75af640a0fa656618523/64655b31-acd1-499f-9ce3-813843bdbe40.png?expires=1794345300&signature=8af46e3b2f0513f31de04e59468f7abb992650832230e32cc4f9897afa622b36&req=dSUjE817mYBbXfMW1HO4zYaKw1rS4lIvXBV0mrsAprjWiKoaUK0suBeCV6Zm%0AgSDIQlD0FNesi2gKaK8%3D%0A)

If your theme meets our requirements, it will be considered for approval. If it lacks any requirements or needs major changes, it will be rejected.

## **Contact Throughout the Review Process**

We may contact you via your registered email during the review. Ensure you receive our emails by adding [email protected] to your email service provider’s allowed sender list.
