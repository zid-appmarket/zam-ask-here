> This article explains how to create and deliver private themes on Zid, focusing on development, testing, and direct merchant upload.
# Private Themes Flow

As a private theme developer, follow these steps to create and deliver a private theme:

## 1. Develop Your Theme

- Utilize the comprehensive Zid theme documentation to build your theme.

- Ensure your theme meets the functional and design requirements of the target merchant.

## 2. Test Your Theme

- Test your theme (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/11461482-test-your-theme) similarly to public themes.

- The main difference is that testing happens within an actual merchant store environment instead of a development store.

## 3. Deliver Theme to Merchant

- Once development and initial testing are complete, coordinate with the merchant to upload the theme.

- The merchant should:

- Log in to their own store dashboard.

- Navigate to the Theme Market (https://web.zid.sa/theme-market).

- Use the **Upload Theme** option to upload the private theme package.

## 4. Merchant Preview and Testing

- The merchant can now preview, activate, and test the theme directly in their store.

- This allows for real-time validation of theme functionality and appearance.

**Note:** The private theme testing process mirrors the public theme testing cycle, but it is conducted directly inside the merchant’s live store rather than a development environment.
