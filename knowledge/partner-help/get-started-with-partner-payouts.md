This article explains how partner payouts work in the Partner Dashboard, including payout records, invoice generation, tax handling, Finance review, and settlement.

The invoice and settlement process depends on the **tax type configured in the approved agreement for each service**.

## **Prerequisites**

- A Zid Partner (https://partners.zid.sa/) account.

- A published paid **App, Theme, or Template** with active transactions.

- The appropriate **payouts permission** to access payout records.

## **Understanding the Payment Process**

The payout process depends on the tax type configured in the approved agreement for each service.

For detailed service-specific guidance, refer to the corresponding payout article:

App Subscription Partners (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/11501759-app-payouts)

Theme Partners (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/17432699-theme-payouts)

Landing Page Partners (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/11791955-landing-page-payouts)

Shipping partners follow a separate settlement process and are **not included in the automatic VAT invoice-generation flow described below**.

## **Automatic VAT Invoice Generation**

For **Apps, Themes, and Templates** whose approved agreement has the **VAT** tax type, Zid automatically generates the invoice. You do **not** need to manually upload an invoice when the VAT payout is first created.

Each invoice covers:

- **One item** — one app, theme, or landing page/template

- **One full calendar month**

For example, if a partner has three VAT-eligible apps, three separate invoices are generated for that month.

## **Invoice Generation Schedule**

### **1st of the month**

- The regular run generates invoices for the **full calendar month that just ended**.

- **Example: **An invoice generated on **1 October** covers **September**.

### **15th of the month**

- The 15th is a **catch-up run**.

- It does **not** cover the first half of the current month.

- Instead, it generates eligible invoices from previous months that were not generated during the regular run.

- For example, this may happen when a VAT agreement is approved after the 1st.

- In many cases, no additional invoice is generated on the 15th.

### **Generation Time**

- The automatic process runs at **03:30 Riyadh time**.

- If the scheduled date falls on a **Friday, Saturday, or national holiday**, invoice generation moves to the **next working day**.

- The invoice date is the date on which the invoice is generated.

## **Payout Flows**

### **VAT — Apps, Themes, and Templates**

For items covered by an approved VAT agreement:

- Zid automatically generates the VAT invoice.

- The payout moves to **Under review**.

- Zid Finance reviews the invoice and payout details.

- Once approved, the payout is processed and moves to **Settled**.

- If Finance identifies an issue:

- The payout is rejected.

- A rejection note is provided.

- The partner receives an email about the rejection.

- The partner can upload a corrected invoice.

The system does not automatically regenerate a corrected invoice after rejection.

### **Non-VAT / WHT Flow**

Items that are not covered by the VAT automatic invoice flow use a separate automatic process.

- The flow runs on the **6th of each month** for the previous month.

- The applicable tax treatment is determined by the item's approved agreement.

- This includes cases where no VAT applies or withholding tax is applicable.

### **Shipping Partners**

- Shipping payouts follow a separate process.

- Zid issues the invoice to the shipping partner. The shipping partner then completes the required payment and uploads the corresponding receipt.

- Shipping partners are **not included** in the automatic VAT invoice-generation flow for Apps, Themes, and Templates.

## **Invoice Requirements**

For VAT-eligible Apps, Themes, and Landing Pages/Templates, Zid generates the initial invoice automatically.

The following invoice requirements remain relevant when reviewing invoice details, for non-VAT/manual cases, or when submitting a **corrected invoice after rejection**.

## **Basic Information**

- Company legal name

- Zid legal name:

- `شركة القدرة التقنية لتقنية المعلومات والاتصالات`

- `Al-Qudrah Al-Taqniyah for Technology and Communication Company`

- CR number and VAT number, if applicable

- Bank account details, preferably for faster settlement

## **Invoice Lines**

- Transactions as individual items or grouped

- Total partner share excluding VAT paid by the merchant

## **Other Conditions**

- Invoice amounts must be in **SAR or USD**, depending on the partner type.

- Partners operating outside KSA are subject to WHT:

- **15% for App partners**

- **5% for Theme partners**

- Partners operating inside KSA with an eligible VAT agreement must include **15% VAT**.

## **Payout Statuses**

**Status**

**Description**

**Pending**

A payout that still requires an action before Finance review. This may apply to flows outside automatic VAT invoice generation.

**Under review / In Review**

Zid Finance is reviewing the invoice and payout. Automatically generated VAT payouts move directly to this stage.

**Rejected**

Finance identified an issue. Review the rejection reason and upload a corrected invoice if required.

**Settled**

The payout has been approved and payment has been processed.

## **Eligible Taxes (VAT & WHT)**

### **Value-Added Tax (VAT)**

- Partners operating in Saudi Arabia must provide an active VAT certificate.

- The company CR must match the provided VAT certificate.

- For eligible items, the VAT amount of **15%** is automatically calculated and added to the invoice.

- The VAT invoice is generated automatically according to the **1st and 15th** schedule.

### **Withholding Tax (WHT)**

Partners operating outside Saudi Arabia are subject to WHT according to the applicable service type:

- **15% for App partners**

- **5% for Theme partners**

The WHT amount is automatically calculated for the payout and deducted from the applicable amount.

## **Missing or Incorrect Invoices**

If an expected invoice is missing or you notice incorrect information:

- Go to **Partner Dashboard → Help & Support → Contact Us**.

- Contact the support team through the available support chat.

- Include the **payout code** so the team can identify the affected payout.

### **If an Invoice Is Missing**

Common reasons include:

- The item is not covered by an approved **VAT** agreement and follows the separate 6th-of-the-month flow.

- The VAT agreement has a **0% rate**.

- The relevant calendar month has not ended yet.

- The payout has already been uploaded, rejected, or settled.

- The payout is for a **Shipping** service, which follows a different process.

### **If an Invoice Is Incorrect**

If Finance identifies an issue:

- Finance rejects the payout and adds a note explaining the reason.

- The partner receives an email about the rejection.

- Review the rejection note in the Partner Dashboard.

- Upload a corrected invoice.

If automatic regeneration is required, it must be handled by the Zid team through the appropriate internal process.
