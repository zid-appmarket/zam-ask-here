If the app type is a shipping or shipping and fulfillment app the webhook will be created automatically from our side all you need is to subscribe to all events from the partner account and fill in the details for the target URL and header, that is it! If the app type is a public or private app you have to create the webhooks needed by using create webhook endpoint (https://docs.zid.sa/docs/zid-merchant-api/so8bnlrdisauw-create-a-webhook)
