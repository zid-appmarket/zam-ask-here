> Learn how to design, build, and publish your landing page templates for Zid merchants.
Landing pages on Zid are single-product promotional pages designed to help merchants highlight specific products or offers. Instead of sending customers to the full storefront, merchants can drive traffic to a focused page built for conversions — ideal for campaigns, seasonal promotions, or spotlighting bestsellers.

As a developer, you can now create and publish landing page **templates** that merchants can browse, purchase, and apply directly from their dashboard. This creates a new opportunity to monetize your work while supporting merchants in their marketing efforts.

## Why you should publish a landing page template

By publishing a landing page template, you can:

- **Offer added value to merchants** looking to boost sales for specific products

- **Expand your reach** within the Zid ecosystem by showing up in the landing page section

- **Monetize your work** through paid templates, with payouts handled through your Partner Dashboard

- **Encourage reusability and customization**, giving merchants a flexible tool without needing custom development

## What’s included in this collection

We will walk you through:

- What makes an effective landing page template

- Design and technical guidelines to meet Zid’s requirements

- How to create, test, and submit your template for approval

- How payouts work once your landing page template is used
