> This article covers the overview of Application Management which enables you to have an overview about App performance.
## Order management

List of your App's orders from Zid stores, with the ability to filter them by Order status, create date, and Delivery date.

(https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/912522732/91ac50da9c0052dcaa235b22/image.png?expires=1794345300&signature=3eebc0f8074f888ce5af0a7bfe73011a2fe429a3f13129111db79c8c93ba9503&req=fSElE8t8moJdFb4f3HP0gGVuxPTKiNEdX7kMYfJfaMHaa5rtUPIjk59oFfgl%0ANtIczgEPvvG8%2FKDcEw%3D%3D%0A)

Also, the main order information will be listed such as:

- Store Id

- Order number

- Tracking number

- Order status

- create date

- Delivery date

- Customer

- Payment

- Total order

- Zid fees

- Settlement status

With an ability to Export them in Excel

## Activations management

This section will display your App activation events by the merchants, with ability to sort them by:

- app Name

- activation status

- settlement status

- Activation date
