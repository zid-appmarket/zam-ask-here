> On this article, you'll find all the needed tips and actions to be applied in your App to reach a successful App publish.
# 1. Prepare your App details

## OAuth Activation

Store information and authorization should be gotten automatically, without any manual work such as X-Manager Token, Store ID

### App Details

#### **App name**

- Should be in both languages (Arabic, English)

#### **App URLs**

- Redirect URL

- Callback URL

- Website URL

- Video URL

- It should be uploaded to YouTube and pasted as an embedded URL.

#### **App Long Description**

- App brief

- App Services

- App features

- Integration Steps

#### **App Short Description**

- One line sentence provides a clear explanation of your App

#### **App logo**

- make sure it's clear enough to be seen and follow this guide:

- Has a background and is viewable from the background.

- In a high-resolution

- If your logo includes an icon, just upload the icon without the name.

#### **App Images**

- make sure it's clear enough to be seen and follow this guide:

- Has a background and is viewable from the background.

- In a high resolution.

- Upload only System user interface or services and features images.

- Screen User interface template in PSD (https://drive.google.com/file/d/17cE-ijX7U7aJTX2xp3P_TU9ql69vlPTc/view?usp=sharing)

- Mobile User interface template in PSD (https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Obo9dX4JRyXTdAt2HpvwVMksVqLq5HEH/view?usp=sharing)

- App webhooks

- Make sure to add the needed App webhooks to mange App functions accurately.

- **[For none shipping App]**

- **App scopes**

- Make sure to choose the needed scopes and specify the reason for each. Note that, each selected scope should be used in the integration as our team will need to test that function.

- **App Plans**

- Make sure to add all the Subscription plans

Check this Example of a great filled App details (https://apps.zid.sa/application/983)

**Tip:** After publishing your app, review its listing in the redesigned Zid App Market to ensure the app description, category placement, subscription plans, and pricing are displayed correctly.

You can preview how merchants discover and compare your app on the Zid App Market (https://apps.zid.sa/).

# 3. Send testing requirements to our team

Congratulations on reaching the milestone of submitting your app for review! This guide will help facilitate a smoother review process and contribute to the successful deployment of your app.

**Post-Submission Steps:**

After submitting your app for review, the next crucial step is to share the testing requirements with our dedicated team. This is essential to initiate the testing phase promptly.

- **Testing Video:** A comprehensive testing video is a key component of the review process. Please ensure that the video covers all specified **testing scenarios (https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/8645309-test-your-application#h_d4647a5e84)** relevant to your app.

- **Providing Test Account Information:** If your app requires an account within your system for testing purposes, kindly provide the necessary details for a test account. This ensures that our team can effectively evaluate the app's functionality.

To do so:

- Contact Message support in the Partner Dashboard

- Click on Technical Concern

- Click on App Testing

- Follow the instructions and provide the needed requirements to proceed with the testing.

- Our team will review your submission and videos

- You'll be contacted in your dashboard to follow up this phase with you.
